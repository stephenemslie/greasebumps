# Watt's The Problem {#generator-problem}

## Start here

Generator given up the ghost? Ware-and-tear-wolves getting you down? Let's find out why!

Let's start the the first things you should always check:

  1. Is there fuel in the tank & correct type?
  2. Is the engine switch on?
  3. Is there enough oil in the engine?
  4. Is the fuel tap open?
  5. Is the spark plug
  6. Inside of tank not rusted
  7. Loose wires (oil sensor, voltage sensor)
